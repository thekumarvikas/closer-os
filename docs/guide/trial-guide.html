1 · What Closer OS is

Closer OS has two parts that work on the same data, live:

📱 Android app — for sales reps

Calls leads from the rep's own phone and SIM, asks for the outcome after every call, plans the next call, sends WhatsApp messages in one tap, reminds follow-ups on time, and uploads call history and recordings automatically.

💻 Website (dashboard) — for management

Leads, team, reports, call log with recordings, follow-up discipline, webinars, sales and revenue, training — and reps can also work from the laptop ("My desk").

Calls are made with the phone's normal dialer and normal SIM — no new numbers, no call charges, no special hardware. Closer OS works alongside the phone app your reps already use.

Who it is for

  • Teams that sell on the phone and WhatsApp: courses, coaching, consulting, real estate, insurance, services.
  • Businesses that run webinars / sales events — reminder calls before, sales calls after.
  • Owners who want to know, every day, who called whom, what happened, and what's next.

Roles

RoleWhat they see and do
Owner / AdminEverything: all leads, all team members, settings, reports, sales, billing.
ManagerTheir team's leads, calls, recordings and reports; assigns leads; reviews calls; verifies sales.
Sales repOnly the leads assigned or shared with them; their own calls, follow-ups and numbers.

2 · Why teams switch to Closer OS

What you get that a regular CRM doesn't give a calling team.

⚡ Built for speed, not data entry

After every call a small card pops up over the phone screen: tap the outcome, tap the next-call time, Save. Two or three taps — the rep never opens a form or types a date.

📞 Every call is captured, automatically

Dialled, received, missed, declined and WhatsApp calls are read from the phone itself — even calls made outside the app. No "I forgot to log it".

🎧 Call recordings without extra cost

Uses the phone's own automatic call recorder. Closer OS finds the file, matches it to the call and uploads it. Managers press ▶ Play.

🔔 Follow-ups that actually happen

Reminders 5 minutes before, on time and after, with a Call button. Every follow-up is marked On time / Late / Not called — visible to management.

🎯 Today's focus list

The app decides who to call first: overdue follow-ups, new leads waiting, webinar reminders, hot leads, best time to call — then calls them one by one with a countdown.

💬 WhatsApp in one tap

Ready messages with the lead's name, webinar date, links and files filled in. WhatsApp queues and multi-day message sequences — without risky auto-sending.

🎓 Made for webinar businesses

Registrations, reminder calls, Zoom attendance, chat and polls, "saw the payment link", hot leads, show-up impact of reminder calls, and sales per webinar.

💰 Real sales, real revenue

Sales are verified by management with full proof (calls, talk time, recordings). Part payments, balance due, buyers list import, days to convert, what buyers have in common.

📴 Works with no internet

Reps keep calling, saving outcomes and notes offline. Everything uploads by itself when the network is back.

🔒 Privacy built in

Only the rep's work SIM is tracked. Personal calls stay on the phone. Recordings are private. Data is stored in India (Mumbai).

In one line: other tools store what reps type in. Closer OS records what reps actually do — and makes them faster while doing it.

3 · Start your trial (owner / manager)

Everything in this section is done on the website, from a laptop or computer.

  1. Sign in. Open the website and click Continue with Google with the Gmail we registered for your company. You land on your company dashboard.
  2. Check company details. Settings → Company: company name, time zone, working days, which calls to track.
  3. Invite your team. Team → Invite: type each person's Gmail and role (Rep / Manager / Admin). They join by signing in with that Gmail — on the website or in the app.
  4. Add your leads — any of these:
    • File: Leads → Import a CSV or Excel file. Closer OS matches the columns for you (a sample file is provided). Duplicate numbers are not added twice — they're marked Repeated.
    • Google Sheet: Lead sources → Google Sheet. Paste our script once; new rows arrive about every minute.
    • Automatic from ads & forms: Lead sources gives a private link for Facebook ads (via Pabbly / Zapier), Flexifunnels, Google Ads forms, website forms, IndiaMART.
  5. Decide who gets which lead. In the lead source: tick the reps who get new leads in turns, or use the sheet's "Team member" column, or assign by hand from the Leads page (select → Assign).
  6. Check your outcomes. Settings → Outcomes: ready-made call results (Interested, Call back later, Didn't pick, Not interested, Sale…) — rename or add your own, and set the default next step for each.
  7. Add WhatsApp messages. Settings → WhatsApp messages: write messages with fill-ins like {{leadName}}, attach brochures/PDFs. Reps send them in one tap.
  8. Send the app link to your reps (next page) and ask them to finish Phone setup on day one.
Optional on day one: set daily targets (Reports → Targets), add your products and prices (Settings → Products & prices), and your UPI / bank details for payment messages (Settings → Payment details).

4 · Install the app (sales reps)

During the trial the app is shared as a Google Drive link (the Play Store version is on its way). Use an Android phone (Android 8 or newer).

Install

  1. Open this Google Drive link on your phone: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1v9f1Y6JPBteTD1acArW4rx1Umn_3R-qP/view?usp=sharing
    Tap the file CloserOS-….apk → Download.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If the phone says "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps", tap Settings → turn on Allow from this source → go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details → Install anyway. (This appears for every app installed outside the Play Store.)
  4. Open Closer OS and tap Continue with Google using the same Gmail your manager invited.

One-time setup (about 5 minutes)

  1. Choose your work SIM. Only calls on this SIM are tracked. Your other SIM stays completely private.
  2. Phone setup checklist (Settings → Open phone setup). Do every step marked NEXT STEP — the app opens the exact Android screen for you:
    • Allow calls, call history, contacts and notifications.
    • Display over other apps — needed for the after-call pop-up.
    • Battery: No restrictions and Autostart — so calls upload even when the app is closed.
    • Automatic call recording in your phone's own Phone app, then choose the recordings folder once.
  3. Make one test call. After it ends, the after-call card should appear. Settings → After-call pop-up → Test the pop-up checks it any time.
Recordings need your phone maker's own Phone app (Samsung, Xiaomi, Vivo, Oppo, Realme, OnePlus…) with auto-record on. If your default Phone app is a downloaded dialer, or Google's Phone app, recordings can't be picked up — the app tells you which case applies.
"Restricted setting" / "App was denied access" when turning on Display over other apps (Android 13+): open App info → ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings, then turn the switch on again. Phone setup has an "Open app info" button for this.

7 · Webinars & sales events

Built for businesses where leads register for a webinar, get reminder calls before it and sales calls after it.

  1. Registrations come in from your sheet / form / import. Closer OS can create the webinar automatically from the sheet's date column (with your default start time) — or you create it yourself.
  2. Reminder calls. Reps get "Reminder calls" lists in the app; calls before the start count as reminder calls for that webinar. The webinar card shows x of y called, N left, per caller.
  3. After the webinar, upload the Zoom reports (attendee report — plus chat and polls if you have them; drop all files at once). Closer OS calculates watch minutes, who was there when the payment link was dropped, chats, poll answers and a warmth score.
  4. Share out sales calls — hottest leads spread evenly among the reps you tick. Hot leads go to the top of everyone's calling list.
  5. See the results: show-up by source (landing page vs direct Zoom link), the effect of reminder calls on show-up, watch-time groups, saw the payment link → called → talked → bought, webinar-by-webinar comparison.
Upload mistakes happen — the webinar page has Undo last upload for 24 hours, and it warns if a Zoom file's date doesn't match the webinar.

8 · Sales, buyers & revenue

  • Sales are verified. A rep saves a "Sale" outcome or claims a sale → the manager sees all the proof on one screen (calls, talk time, WhatsApp, recordings, webinar watched, red flags like very short talk or a duplicate) → Verify or Reject. Only verified sales count in reports.
  • Payments ledger. Full, part payments and refunds (UPI, bank, cash, card…). Orders stay "Balance due" until fully paid. Lock a month when accounts are closed.
  • Buyers list. Import your buyers file (CSV / Excel) or connect a Google Sheet. Closer OS cleans messy numbers and emails, matches each buyer to the lead, marks them Bought in everyone's app, and lists anything it couldn't match for you to check.
  • Sales page: revenue, sales, collected, pending, how long buyers take to pay (from sign-up or from the webinar), by team member, by webinar, by product, what buyers have in common, missed money (hot leads not bought, attendees never called).
  • Each buyer's journey — every registration, webinar, call, recording, WhatsApp and payment on one timeline.
  • Training: a library of the calls that led to real sales — play or download, to train new reps.
  • In the app: reps see "My Sales Revenue" for the month.

9 · Your data & privacy

QuestionAnswer
Where is the data stored?In India — a secure cloud database in Mumbai.
Are my personal calls uploaded?Only calls on the work SIM are tracked. The other SIM's calls never appear anywhere in Closer OS. The company can also choose to track only calls with leads.
Who can hear recordings?Recordings are private files. Only people who can see that lead (the rep, their managers, admins) can play them, and every play/download is logged.
What can reps see?Only their own assigned or shared leads and their own numbers.
One phone per loginA login works on one phone at a time; signing in elsewhere signs out the old phone after uploading its data.
Does Closer OS staff see our data?Only if you allow support access (Settings), and every support visit is recorded and visible to you. You can turn it off.
WhatsAppCloser OS never sends WhatsApp messages by itself — the rep taps Send — so your numbers stay safe from WhatsApp bans.
