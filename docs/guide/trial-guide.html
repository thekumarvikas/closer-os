Getting started

Owner / manager — on the website

  1. Sign in with Google using the Gmail we registered for you.
  2. Add your team by their Gmail addresses.
  3. Bring in your leads — Excel, Google Sheet or your lead forms. We'll help connect them.
  4. Share the app link with your team.

Sales rep — on the Android phone

  1. Download the app: Google Drive link. If the phone asks, allow installing from this source.
  2. Sign in with Google using the Gmail your manager added.
  3. Pick your work SIM and follow the short phone setup — the app opens each setting for you.
  4. Start calling.

Tip: on the first day, make one test call to see the after-call card. For call recordings, use your phone maker's own Phone app with automatic recording switched on.
